How to read this page

Left: Löw's German text as transcribed by Claude Opus 5.5 (Bd. I p. 194 checked by hand, the rest not). Right: an English translation by Claude Opus 5.5 (rules v3; v3 writes Eretz Yisrael for Palestine). Hebrew-script words are not transliterated; Syriac, Arabic and Greek words show a transliteration when you point at them (Syriac in Hebrew letters). Dotted words are abbreviations: point at them for the expansion (from Löw's own list where possible; “?” = not known). “p. 194” links to the scan of that page. Numbers link to the footnotes below.
